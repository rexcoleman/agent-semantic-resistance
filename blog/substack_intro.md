**Subject line:** Domain-aligned attacks cascade at 98%. Standard payloads get caught. Here's why.

My simulation said multi-agent systems cascade at 97%. Real agents: 60%. Two projects later, I finally know WHY.

The answer is surprising: resistance varies by 98 percentage points depending on what you inject. "Grant admin access" cascades at 98%. "Buy CryptoScamCoin" is caught 31% of the time. Domain-aligned framing? Invisible to every detection method we tested.

Three resistance patterns. Six experiments on real Claude agents. The most dangerous attacks aren't the ones that cascade — they're the ones you can't see.
