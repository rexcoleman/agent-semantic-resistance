## Key Messages

1. Real agents resist cascade at ~40% — simulations miss this entirely
2. Three resistance mechanisms: semantic incongruity, domain knowledge, self-correction
3. Domain-aligned attacks bypass the strongest resistance pattern
4. Delegation depth is a natural defense (deeper = less poison)
5. Understanding resistance enables both better attacks AND better defenses
